Synth Controls

Play notes using your keyboard or a connected MIDI controller (25-key layout assumed).

Keyboard Mapping:

  • Lower Octave: ASDFGHJK L;'
  • Upper Octave: QWERTYUIOP []

Adjust settings:

  • Change Octave: Z (down), X (up)
  • Change Key: Left Arrow (down), Right Arrow (up)

Use the controls panel (top-right) to modify the sound (envelope, waveform, volume) and visuals.

MIDI velocity affects the initial size of the visual.
